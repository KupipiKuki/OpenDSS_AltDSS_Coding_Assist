## Edge checks

- Both classic interfaces expose `NumCktElements`. DSS-Python uses `Totaliterations` and OpenDSSDirect.py uses `TotalIterations()`. OpenDSSDirect.py exposes the solution tolerance as `Convergence()`.
- The OpenDSSDirect.py active-class walk returned 9, 9 and 9 for `RegControls.TapNumber()` on IEEE 13. Named text queries returned 9, 6 and 9. After explicitly selecting reg2 in an isolated probe, `TapNumber(7)` returned `None` and changed that tap from 6 to 7. The tool uses text queries for both classic readbacks.
- The loss-scale guard accepted 112.391709 kW and rejected the unconverted 112,391.709 W value when paired with IEEE 13 source kW. The engine guard rejected a 0.14.50 version string.
- Reordering the same named records preserved agreement. Voltage and power differences within tolerance remained visible in `nonzero_deltas`. An out-of-tolerance angle and changed iteration and tap integers failed the comparison.
- Injecting a 0.01 pu difference at IEEE 13 bus 611 in the OpenDSSDirect.py worker readback produced exit code 1. The temporary report identified the bus, magnitude index, and two affected wrapper pairs. The third pair still passed.
